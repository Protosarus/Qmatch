## Contact

Email: **support@qmatch.app** (**NEEDS CONFIRMATION** — mailbox must receive mail, send replies, and be monitored).

Need more help?

Email us at support@qmatch.app

We read every message. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account (processed within 30 days), or email support with the phone or email on your account.

## Account deletion

Go to Settings → Delete account, read the notices, confirm both checkboxes, type DELETE, and submit. We process requests within 30 days. You can also email support@qmatch.app.

More detail: [Account deletion](https://qmatch.app/account-deletion)
