We read every message. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account (processed within 30 days), or email support with the phone or email on your account.

Account deletion

Go to Settings → Delete account, read the notices, confirm both checkboxes, type DELETE, and submit. We process requests within 30 days. You can also email support@qmatch.site.
